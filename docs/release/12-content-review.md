## Claims a human must confirm before release

1. **APS for Pakistan.** No official source supports a requirement. Confirm in writing with DAAD Islamabad or the embassy.
2. **Blocked-account amount.** 992 EUR per month from 1 January 2025. Confirm it is still current at release. Check whether applicants without an admission letter need a different amount; a secondary source mentioned 1,091 EUR for another mission.
3. **Visa process.** Catchment areas, the portal, and the full document list, including language proof and conditional admission. The embassy checklist PDFs could not be read.
4. **Tuition rules.** Baden-Württemberg and Bavaria change by state law.
5. **uni-assist certified-copy rules,** which differ per university.
6. **IELTS thresholds,** which vary per programme.
7. **Appointment waiting times.** The guide stays vague on purpose.
8. **Unreadable sources.** make-it-in-germany.com and britishcouncil.pk could not be fetched; check them by hand.

The guide states no work-hour limits, deadlines with dates, or fee amounts beyond those above. Adding any of them needs the same review.
