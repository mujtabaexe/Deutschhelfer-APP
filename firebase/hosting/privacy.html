Privacy policy

Deutschhelfer, by Sahibzada Ahmad Mujtaba · Last updated 2026-10-27

This policy matches the privacy notice inside the app. Questions: sahibzadaahmadmujtaba@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address and password, to create and secure your account. Google stores the password as a hash; we never see it.
  • Your display name, which other members can see.
  • What you post: group messages, private messages, and reports you file.
  • Group memberships, people you block, and when you accepted the community rules.
  • If you turn on notifications: a push token for your device.
  • If you subscribe: the purchase record from Google Play.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos or files. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell your data.

Why we use it

To run your account, show your messages to the people you send them to, keep the community safe through reports and moderation, send the notifications you turned on, and unlock purchased content.

Who processes it

The app runs on Google Firebase (authentication, database, server functions and push notifications), in Google's Mumbai, India (asia-south1) region. Google processes the data on our behalf, together with technical data such as IP addresses and device integrity checks that protect the service from abuse. Purchases go through Google Play.

Who can see what

Your email address is visible only to you. Group members see your display name and group messages. Private messages are visible only to you and the other person; moderators cannot read them. Moderators see reported content.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by server-side security rules, and moderator actions are logged.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you delete it, your name is replaced with “Deleted user” at once, you leave every group, your sign-in is switched off, and your blocks and notification tokens are removed. Your sign-in, profile, reports you filed and subscription records are erased after 90 days. Messages you sent stay visible in the group or conversation under “Deleted user”. Reports other people filed about your messages are kept for community safety. Moderation logs keep a pseudonymous account ID; their free-text notes are removed after 90 days. See how to delete your account.

Your choices and rights

In the app you can change your display name, turn notifications off, block people, withdraw your group messages and delete your account. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data or its deletion.

Children

Deutschhelfer is for students preparing for university. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and the app asks you to confirm this when you sign up. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly keep data about them. If you believe a person under 18 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page and the in-app notice together, and change the date above.
