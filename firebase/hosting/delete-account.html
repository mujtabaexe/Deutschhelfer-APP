Delete your Deutschhelfer account

Deutschhelfer, by Sahibzada Ahmad Mujtaba · Last updated 2026-10-27

How to ask for deletion

In the app: open Profile, tap Delete account, then confirm with your password.

Without the app: send an email to sahibzadaahmadmujtaba@gmail.com from the email address you used for your account, with the subject “Delete my Deutschhelfer account”. We reply within 7 days. We will email a one-time code to your account's address and act only when you reply with it, so nobody else can delete your account.

If you subscribe through Google Play, also cancel the subscription in the Play Store. Deleting your account does not cancel Google Play billing.

What is deleted

  • Straight away: your display name is replaced with “Deleted user”, you leave every group, your sign-in is switched off, and your blocks and notification tokens are removed.
  • After 90 days: your sign-in record, profile, reports you filed and subscription records are erased.

What is kept

  • Messages you sent stay in the group or conversation under “Deleted user”, so the conversation still makes sense for the other members.
  • Moderation logs keep a pseudonymous account ID, to show what moderators did. Their free-text notes are removed after 90 days.
  • Reports other people filed about your messages are kept for community safety.

To have your past messages removed as well, say so in your email.
